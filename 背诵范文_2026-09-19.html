Directions: Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should 1) interpret the chart, and 2) give your comments. You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

手机15%72%↑ 57 个百分点
电视55%18%↓ 37 个百分点

范文全文（165 词）

Para 1 描述图表The chart above illustrates a dramatic shift in how residents of a certain city accessed news between 2019 and 2025. During this period, the proportion of people getting news on mobile phones surged from 15% to 72%, whereas television and newspapers, once dominant, dropped to 18% and 10% respectively.

译：上图显示了 2019 至 2025 年间某市居民获取新闻方式的显著转变。在此期间，用手机获取新闻的人群占比从 15% 飙升至 72%，而曾占主导地位的电视与报纸则分别降至 18% 和 10%。

Para 2 分析原因Several factors contribute to this trend. First and foremost, smartphones have made news available anytime and anywhere, which perfectly matches the fast pace of modern life. Moreover, news apps and short-video platforms deliver information that is both timely and personalized, an advantage that traditional media can hardly offer. Last but not least, younger generations, who were born into the digital era, naturally prefer screens to paper.

译：若干因素促成了这一趋势。首先，智能手机让人们随时随地都能看到新闻，这与现代生活的快节奏完美契合。其次，新闻客户端与短视频平台推送的信息既及时又个性化，这是传统媒体难以提供的优势。最后，出生于数字时代的年轻一代天然更偏爱屏幕而非纸张。

Para 3 结论预测Taking all these factors into account, we may safely conclude that mobile devices will continue to dominate news consumption in the years to come. For traditional media, the key to survival lies in embracing digital transformation rather than clinging to old formats.

译：综合考虑这些因素，我们有把握地得出结论：未来几年移动设备将持续主导新闻消费。对传统媒体而言，生存的关键在于拥抱数字化转型，而非固守旧有形式。

三段式骨架（背结构，不是背句子）

段落功能句型骨架
Para 1描述图表总述变化 → 点出最大数据的升降（whereas 对比）
Para 2分析原因总起句 → First and foremost → Moreover → Last but not least（3 个原因）
Para 3结论/建议预测趋势（will continue to…）→ 给一方建议（the key lies in…）

换话题三步：① 改图表名词（网购/健身/考研人数…）② 原因三选三 ③ 结论方向随数据涨跌调整。骨架不动，任何图表题都能套。

必背套句 × 6（点开核对中文，先遮后背）

1 / 6 · 图表总述句
The chart above illustrates a dramatic shift in … between YEAR1 and YEAR2.
上图显示了 …… 年至 …… 年间……的显著变化。｜ 把 shift 换成 increase / decline 即可写上升/下降题。
2 / 6 · 数据对比句
During this period, the proportion of … surged from A% to B%, whereas … dropped to C% and D% respectively.
此期间，……的占比从 A% 飙升至 B%，而 …… 则分别降至 C% 和 D%。｜ surged→climbed / rocketed；dropped→fell / declined。
3 / 6 · 原因总起句
Several factors contribute to this trend.
若干因素促成了这一趋势。｜ 也可用 This trend can be attributed to the following factors.
4 / 6 · 三层递进句
First and foremost, … . Moreover, … . Last but not least, … .
首先……；此外……；最后但同样重要的是……。｜ 每层各写一个原因，第二层可加 which 定语从句拉长度。
5 / 6 · 结论句
Taking all these factors into account, we may safely conclude that … will continue to … in the years to come.
综合考虑上述因素，我们可以稳妥地得出结论：……在未来几年将继续……。
6 / 6 · 建议句
For …, the key to … lies in … rather than … .
对……而言，……的关键在于……而非……。｜ 万能收尾，任何话题都能给出立场。
