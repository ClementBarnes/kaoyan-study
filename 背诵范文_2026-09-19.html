范文全文（190 词 · 17 个本批词已入文高亮）

Para 1 描述图表As a recent government report indicates, the way residents of a certain city transport themselves has undergone a dramatic shift between 2019 and 2025: the proportion of subway commuters surged from 25% to 48%, whereas private-car trips, once dominant, dropped to 32%.

译：正如政府近期一份报告所指出的，2019 至 2025 年间，某市居民的出行方式发生了显著转变：地铁通勤者占比从 25% 飙升至 48%，而曾占主导地位的私家车出行则降至 32%。

Para 2 分析原因Several factors contribute to this trend. First and foremost, the municipal government has committed itself to reshaping the structure of urban mobility, constructing three new subway lines and permitting private cars downtown only on weekends. Moreover, the rise of the subway does not contradict green goals: unlike cars that destroy the environment, it offers the punctual service that daily commuters expect. Last but not least, from the perspective of long-term development, every aspect of city planning now shows greater respect for nature, opening bright prospects for a car-light future.

译：若干因素促成了这一趋势。首先，市政府致力于重塑城市出行的结构——新建三条地铁线，并规定仅周末允许私家车进入市中心。其次，地铁的兴起并不违背绿色目标：与破坏环境的私家车不同，它提供了日常通勤者所期待的准点服务。最后，从长远发展的视角看，城市规划的方方面面都体现出对自然更多的尊重，为「轻车未来」开辟了光明前景。

Para 3 结论预测Experts predict that by 2030 more than half of all trips will be made by subway. Taking all these factors into account, we may safely conclude that the key to a greener city lies in making public transit attractive rather than forcing change — a mission that requires both wise planning and continuous public support.

译：专家预测，到 2030 年全市超过一半的出行将由地铁承担。综合考虑这些因素，我们可以稳妥地得出结论：建设更绿色城市的钥匙在于让公共交通更有吸引力，而非强行改变——这一使命既需要明智的规划，也需要持续的支持。

未入文 10 词 · 同场景例句（地铁线连载，背词=背故事）

词例句（续「某市地铁」剧情）释义
inspectEngineers inspect the new subway lines every week.工程师每周检查新地铁线。（in+spect 进去看=检查）
instructThe mayor instructed the transport bureau to cut waiting times.市长指示交通局缩短候车时间。
importThe city imports advanced equipment for its rail projects.该市为轨道项目进口先进设备。
exportA strong transit network helps local firms export goods faster.便捷的交通网帮本地企业更快出口货物。
portablePortable tickets — just tap your phone — replace paper ones.便携车票（手机一碰）取代了纸质票。
dictateBudget, not wishful thinking, dictates how fast lines can be built.预算而非空想决定线路建多快。
dedicateThe building team dedicated five years to the project.建设团队为该项目奉献了五年。
admitOfficials admit that rush hours are still crowded.官员们承认高峰期仍然拥挤。
submitResidents submitted over 2,000 suggestions for the plan.居民为规划提交了两千多条建议。
dismissDo not dismiss small delays; they add up.别对小的延误不以为然——它们会累积。

这 10 句连起来就是「地铁线从规划到通车的完整故事」：市长指示 → 工程师检查 → 进口设备 → 居民提交建议 → 团队奉献施工 → 官员承认问题 → 手机票取代纸质票 → 别小看延误 → 交通网助出口。背故事 = 背单词。

三段式骨架（背结构，不是背句子）

段落功能句型骨架
Para 1描述图表总述变化 → 点出最大数据的升降（whereas 对比）
Para 2分析原因总起句 → First and foremost → Moreover → Last but not least（3 个原因）
Para 3结论/建议预测趋势（will continue to…）→ 给一方建议（the key lies in…）

换话题三步：① 改图表名词（网购/健身/考研人数…）② 原因三选三 ③ 结论方向随数据涨跌调整。骨架不动，任何图表题都能套。

必背套句 × 6（点开核对中文，先遮后背）

1 / 6 · 图表总述句
The chart above illustrates a dramatic shift in … between YEAR1 and YEAR2.
上图显示了 …… 年至 …… 年间……的显著变化。｜ 把 shift 换成 increase / decline 即可写上升/下降题。
2 / 6 · 数据对比句
During this period, the proportion of … surged from A% to B%, whereas … dropped to C% and D% respectively.
此期间，……的占比从 A% 飙升至 B%，而 …… 则分别降至 C% 和 D%。｜ surged→climbed / rocketed；dropped→fell / declined。
3 / 6 · 原因总起句
Several factors contribute to this trend.
若干因素促成了这一趋势。｜ 也可用 This trend can be attributed to the following factors.
4 / 6 · 三层递进句
First and foremost, … . Moreover, … . Last but not least, … .
首先……；此外……；最后但同样重要的是……。｜ 每层各写一个原因，第二层可加 which 定语从句拉长度。
5 / 6 · 结论句
Taking all these factors into account, we may safely conclude that … will continue to … in the years to come.
综合考虑上述因素，我们可以稳妥地得出结论：……在未来几年将继续……。
6 / 6 · 建议句
For …, the key to … lies in … rather than … .
对……而言，……的关键在于……而非……。｜ 万能收尾，任何话题都能给出立场。
