Paragraph 1（4 句 · 84 词）
P1

1.1 The longest bull run in a century of art-market history ended on a dramatic note with a sale of 56 works by Damien Hirst, "Beautiful Inside My Head Forever", at Sotheby's in London on September 15th 2008 (see pictrue).

1.2 All but two pieces sold, fetching more than £70m, a record for a sale by a single artist.

1.3 It was a last hurrah.

1.4 As the auctioneer called out bids, in New York one of the oldest banks on Wall Street, Lehman Brothers, filed for bankruptcy.

📖 点击展开本段中译（建议先自己写）
艺术市场历史上持续时间最长的牛市，在一个戏剧性的节点上结束了：2008年9月15日，伦敦苏富比拍卖行拍卖了达明·赫斯特的56件作品《我脑海中的美丽永存》。56件中除两件外全部售出，成交额超过7000万英镑，创下单一位艺术家拍卖的纪录。这是一场最后的欢呼。就在拍卖师喊出报价的同时，在纽约，华尔街最古老的银行之一——雷曼兄弟——申请了破产。
Paragraph 2（4 句 · 82 词）
P2

2.1 The world art market had already been losing momentum for a while after rising vertiginously since 2003.

2.2 At its peak in 2007 it was worth some $65 billion, reckons Clare McAndrew, founder of Arts Economics, a research firm—double the figure five years earlier.

2.3 Since then it may have come down to $50 billion.

2.4 But the market generates interest far beyond its size because it brings together great wealth, enormous egos, greed, passion and controversy in a way matched by few other industries.

📖 点击展开本段中译（建议先自己写）
自2003年以令人眩晕的速度攀升之后，世界艺术市场其实已经失去动力一段时间了。2007年其峰值约为650亿美元（艺术经济学研究公司创始人克莱尔·麦克安德鲁这样估计）——是五年前的两倍。此后可能已降至500亿美元。但这个市场产生的关注度远超其体量，因为它以别无仅有的方式把巨大的财富、膨胀的自我、贪婪、激情和争议集于一堂。
Paragraph 3（4 句 · 112 词）
P3

3.1 In the weeks and months that followed Mr Hirst's sale, spending of any sort became deeply unfashionable, especially in New York, where the bail-out of the banks coincided with the loss of thousands of jobs and the financial demise of many art-buying investors.

3.2 In the art world that meant collectors stayed away from galleries and salerooms.

3.3 Sales of contemporary art fell by two-thirds, and in the most overheated sector—for Chinese contemporary art—they were down by nearly 90% in the year to November 2008.

3.4 Within weeks the world's two biggest auction houses, Sotheby's and Christie's, had to pay out nearly $200m in guarantees to clients who had placed works for sale with them.

📖 点击展开本段中译（建议先自己写）
在赫斯特作品拍卖之后的数周数月里，任何形式的消费都变得极不时髦，在纽约尤其如此——在那里，银行救助 coincides 伴随着 Thousands of 工作岗位的丧失和许多艺术买家投资者的财务消亡。在艺术界，这意味着收藏家们不再去画廊和拍卖行。当代艺术销售额下降了三分之二，而在最过热板块——中国当代艺术——截至2008年11月的一年内跌幅接近90%。几周之内，全球最大的两家拍卖行苏富比和佳士得不得不向寄售作品的客户支付了近2亿美元的担保金。
Paragraph 4（3 句 · 73 词）
P4

4.1 The current downturn in the art market is the worst since the Japanese stopped buying Impressionists at the end of 1989, a move that started the most serious contraction in the market since the second world war.

4.2 This time experts reckon that prices are about 40% down on their peak on average, though some have been far more volatile.

4.3 But Edward Dolman, Christie's chief executive, says: "I'm pretty confident we're at the bottom."

📖 点击展开本段中译（建议先自己写）
当前艺术市场的下滑，是自1989年末日本人停止购买印象派作品以来最严重的一次——当时的举动引发了二战以来该市场最严重的一次收缩。专家估计这一次价格平均比峰值下跌约40%，不过某些品种的波动要剧烈得多。但佳士得首席执行官爱德华·多尔曼说：我相当有信心我们到底了。
Paragraph 5（5 句 · 118 词）
P5

5.1 What makes this slump different from the last, he says, is that there are still buyers in the market, whereas in the early 1990s, when interest rates were high, there was no demand even though many collectors wanted to sell.

5.2 Christie's revenues in the first half of 2009 were still higher than in the first half of 2006.

5.3 Almost everyone who was interviewed for this special report said that the biggest problem at the moment is not a lack of demand but a lack of good work to sell.

5.4 The three Ds—death, debt and divorce—still deliver works of art to the market.

5.5 But anyone who does not have to sell is keeping away, waiting for confidence to return.

📖 点击展开本段中译（建议先自己写）
他说，这次低迷与上一次的不同之处在于，市场上仍有买家；而在20世纪90年代初利率高企时，即便许多收藏家想卖，也没有需求。佳士得2009年上半年的营收仍高于2006年上半年。为这篇特别报道受访的几乎所有人都说，眼下最大的问题不是缺乏需求，而是缺乏可卖的好作品。三个D——死亡、债务和离婚——仍不断把艺术品送进市场。但凡不是非卖不可的人都在观望，等待信心回归。

四、长难句拆解 · 3 句

第1段 · 现在分词作状语 + 双谓语并列
As the auctioneer called out bids, in New York one of the oldest banks on Wall Street, Lehman Brothers, filed for bankruptcy.
拆：as 引导的时间状语从句里主语是 the auctioneer（拍卖师喊价）；主句主语 one of the oldest banks on Wall Street 被逗号切开，后面接同位语 Lehman Brothers，再跟谓语 filed for bankruptcy。译：就在拍卖师喊出报价时，纽约华尔街最老的银行之一雷曼兄弟申请了破产。
第3段 · 破折号插入 + 复杂名词堆叠
Sales of contemporary art fell by two-thirds, and in the most overheated sector—for Chinese contemporary art—they were down by nearly 90% in the year to November 2008.
拆：主句 Sales of contemporary art fell by two-thirds；and 后是并列句，主语 they 指代前面的 Sales；破折号内for Chinese contemporary art 是插入语补充说明 sector。译：当代艺术销售额下降了三分之二，而在最过热的板块——中国当代艺术——截至2008年11月的一年内跌幅接近90%。
第5段 · 插入语 + 定语从句 + 让步状语从句
What makes this slump different from the last, he says, is that there are still buyers in the market, whereas in the early 1990s, when interest rates were high, there was no demand even though many collectors wanted to sell.
拆：主干 = What makes this slump different from the last is that there are still buyers in the market；he says 是插入语；whereas 引导对比状语从句，从句里再嵌 when interest rates were high 时间状语和 even though 引导的让步状语。译：他说，这次低迷与上次不同之处在于市场上仍有买家；而在90年代初利率高企时，即便许多收藏家想卖也没有需求。
铁律：先找主干（主+谓+宾）→ 再挂修饰 → 最后才处理插入语和从句。任何句子 3 遍内拆不开就别硬拆，标进错题本。明天起从真题原文挑真句练。
